## Motion and transitions

Make motion a prominent part of the presentation. Give every slide a deliberate entrance, a visual build, and a transition that supports its meaning. Use moving confidence markers, progressive learning-loop segments, staggered feature reveals, an advancing roadmap marker, and animated budget allocations.

Keep titles and essential numbers still once revealed so the presenter can explain them. Use 0.4–0.7 second entrances, roughly 0.1–0.2 second stagger between related elements, and 0.4–0.8 second slide transitions. Prefer Match & Move where supported, directional reveals, wipes, gentle pans, and short fades. Use the closest available Canva effect if an exact effect is unavailable. Do not claim unsupported animations were applied.

Avoid flashing, spinning text, repeated bouncing, or zooming through dense tables. Use presenter-controlled builds for substantive content. Include a static PDF export option where all information remains understandable without animation.
